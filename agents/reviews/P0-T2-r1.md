## Security review

Explicitly reviewed budget bypass/overshoot, identity mapping, audit trust boundaries, and database authority as required by the plan. TokenReview-derived identity, gateway audience validation, ignoring client identity, and runtime validation of Tenant/Agent objects are the right boundary; tenant labels and ServiceAccount naming must remain controller-owned and protected in later RBAC/admission work. Namespace names/labels alone do not prove isolation, and ADR-003 correctly states that limit.

Agent and tenant counters must remain atomic across replicas and settlement; uncertain consumption cannot be refunded repeatedly. The counter-store technology may remain undecided, but findings 1–2 must constrain any choice. UTC windows and admission-window settlement preserve the accepted reset semantics; their unconditional cap claims still need correction.

Audit writer privileges exclude UPDATE/DELETE/TRUNCATE; migration authority is separated. Checkpoints bind a tenant, head hash, count and timestamp to an out-of-database anchor, with trusted keys obtained out of band. The uncheckpointed tail, privileged suffix rewriting, and logging-completeness limitations are candidly documented. The signed-byte contract and fail-closed delivery guarantees need correction before approval (findings 3–4). Phase 0 remains format-only.

The six accepted owner choices are traced to the approved plan and are not reopened here. Unresolved counter storage, platform-chain attribution, tenant RPM, burst sizing, encoding/algorithm details, and retention remain owner/specification questions, not accepted decisions inferred by this review. Restricted local PostgreSQL is also required by the plan/requirements; calling that owner-accepted should not be interpreted as approval of the proposed role/trigger implementation.
