## Security review

Rechecked the plan's sensitive boundaries: budget bypass/overshoot, token-derived identity, audit trust and restricted database writes. This revision closes the retry double-counting gap without weakening the chain or granting UPDATE/DELETE. Stable event identity is itself hashed; cross-tenant lookup/uniqueness is scoped by `chain_id`; conflicting payloads fail rather than silently replacing history. Pending payloads must remain durable across crashes as specified.

The documents correctly describe effectively-once audit effects through retry-safe delivery, not an atomic transaction spanning counter and audit stores. Later implementation must preserve atomic settlement plus pending-event creation and must not clear pending obligations before confirmed append-or-existing success. Unique-constraint races must be retried/read under the same append semantics, not suppressed as successful delivery without checking content.

Hard consumption and execution bounds, counter-store atomicity, trusted backend usage, epoch fencing and inflight release remain explicit assumptions and future implementation obligations. Signed checkpoint metadata, external verification keys, tenant binding and the uncheckpointed-tail limitation are unchanged. Audit admission precedes forwarding; the already-delivered-stream limitation remains explicit. No cloud resources, secrets or real model-provider tests were introduced.
